Valuation of Practice for Purchase / Sale / Buy-in Negotiation of Terms / Expert Witness & Litigation Support

We will perform a valuation of your practice to estimate its financial worth in today’s changing healthcare marketplace. Valuations or Appraisals are needed for the following:

  • Sale of the practice
  • Buy-out of partnership
  • Buy-ins to partnership
  • Buying a practice
  • Mergers to equalize assets
  • Sales to integrated delivery systems, practice management companies, management services organizations
  • Property tax or insurance
  • Divorce Valuation

 

The comprehensive written report outlines the factors and methodologies—utilizing at least six different approaches—used to arrive at the final recommended figure. The valuation consists of either an on-site review or, for non-local practices, the use of digital pictures and descriptions (including make/model numbers) of all tangible assets, along with an analysis of the financial information. A written report is provided with the rationale for valuation, including a comparison to norms by specialty. Reports are delivered electronically via email or secure file-sharing platforms for ease of distribution to potential buyers.

In addition, advice can be provided on structuring the sale or purchase and negotiating terms. Assistance in acquiring legal services can also be arranged through our network of experts.
